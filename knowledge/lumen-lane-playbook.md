# Lumen Lane Digital: agency playbook (for Volty)

> Lumen Lane Digital is a fictional agency, set up as a test client for a Volty agent build. Every name, price and result below is made up.

## Who we are

- Performance marketing and content agency in Singapore (Tanjong Pagar), founded 2019, team of 24.
- We work with consumer brands and service businesses in Singapore and Malaysia: DTC/e-commerce, clinics and healthcare, F&B groups, education providers, B2B SaaS.
- Positioning: "Marketing you can measure." Every retainer reports on revenue or leads, not just reach.
- Hours: Mon–Fri, 9:00–18:00 SGT. Strategists reply to qualified enquiries within 1 working day.

## Services and starting prices (SGD, before GST)

| Service | What it covers | Starting price |
|---|---|---|
| Paid ads | Meta, Google, TikTok and LinkedIn ads: strategy, creative, setup, weekly optimisation, monthly report | From S$3,500/month management fee + ad spend (minimum ad spend S$5,000/month recommended) |
| SEO | Technical audit, content plan, on-page fixes, link building, monthly ranking report | From S$2,800/month, 6-month minimum |
| Social media | Content calendar, 12–20 posts/month, community management, light paid boosting | From S$3,200/month |
| Content | Blog articles, email newsletters, video scripts, case studies | From S$1,800/month (4 long-form pieces) |
| Website & branding | Brand identity, Webflow/Shopify website, landing pages | Projects from S$15,000 (brand) and S$18,000 (website) |

- Bundles: most clients combine Paid ads with Social media or Content. A two-service bundle is 10% off the combined management fees.
- Ad spend is paid by the client straight to the platform, never through us.
- All prices are starting points. **Only a strategist gives a final quote**, after a discovery call.

## Who we are a great fit for (ideal client profile)

- Budget: total marketing budget (fees + ad spend) of **S$8,000/month or more**, or a one-off project of S$15,000+.
- Timeline: wants to start **within 3 months**.
- Decision: the person writing is the decision maker or is speaking for them (founder, CEO, CMO, head of marketing).
- Need: a service from the table above.

## Not a fit (be honest and kind, suggest an alternative where we can)

- Budgets under S$3,000/month in total: suggest our free resources (below) and come back when ready.
- Services we do not offer: mobile app development, printing, event management, PR/media relations, influencer-only campaigns, crypto/gambling/adult products (policy).

## Guarantees and discounts

- We never guarantee results ("page 1 on Google", "10x ROAS"), because rankings and ad performance depend on platforms and markets we don't control. We share past client results as examples instead.
- Discounts and custom pricing are decided only by a strategist.
- A prospect who **asks** for a guarantee or a discount is still a prospect: it does not make them Not a fit. Explain kindly, then hand them to a strategist as a pricing conversation.

## Process after a qualified enquiry

1. Volty qualifies the enquiry and records it in the Leads table.
2. Hot leads are handed to a strategist the same working day; the strategist emails within 1 working day to book a 30-minute discovery call.
3. Discovery call → proposal within 5 working days → 2-week onboarding → kickoff.
4. Contracts: retainers run month to month after the minimum term (SEO 6 months, others 3 months), with 30 days' notice.

## Proof points (anonymised client results, fictional)

- DTC skincare brand: cut cost per purchase by 38% in 4 months on Meta + TikTok ads.
- Dental clinic group (3 outlets): 2.4x organic leads in 6 months from SEO.
- B2B SaaS: 61 sales-qualified leads in a quarter from LinkedIn ads + content.

## Free resources for nurture leads

- "The S$5k/month Paid Ads Starter Guide" (PDF, emailed on request by a strategist).
- Monthly newsletter "Lumen Notes": tips on ads, SEO and social for SG/MY businesses.

## FAQs

- **Do you work outside Singapore?** Yes, Singapore and Malaysia, and regional campaigns across SEA for existing clients.
- **Do you lock clients into long contracts?** No: a 3-month minimum (6 for SEO), then month to month.
- **Who owns the ad accounts and content?** The client, always.
- **Can I see a sample report?** A strategist shares one on the discovery call.
- **Do you offer free audits?** A free 20-minute ads or SEO audit is part of the discovery call for qualified leads.
- **What does GST look like?** 9% GST is added to all fees.
